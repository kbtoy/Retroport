Basket

Item stats

Install makes every piece a level-1 cosmetic copy with no stats. Give them real ones here: set what all pieces share, fill in the stats, then adjust any piece - the tooltip shows it as it will look in game. Numbers follow WotLK's own items.

Item sets

A set's pieces show it in their tooltip, and wearing enough of them gives its bonuses - spells your 3.3.5 client already has (every bonus of its own item sets), or any spell by its id. Install doesn't put item sets in the game yet: they need rows in the client's and the server's item set tables.

Welcome

Point the workbench at the 32-bit client (the folder with WowClient32.exe) and at your own retail install: Install reads retail's items, models and textures from there and puts them into the client as they are - nothing is converted. Browsing works from wago.tools until then.

Install

Install puts the basket's items and mounts into the 32-bit client as retail has them - its own models, textures and tables, nothing converted - and writes their SQL for your server. It backs up what it replaces, and Undo puts it back.
